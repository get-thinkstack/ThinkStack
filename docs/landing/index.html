Offline research assistant — ingest papers, search, chat, and write LaTeX. All locally, all private. No cloud, no accounts.

Download for x64 Download .deb (Debian/Ubuntu)

Minimum Requirements

glibc ≥ 2.28, glibcxx ≥ 3.4.25 (e.g. Ubuntu 20, Debian 10, Fedora 36, RHEL 8). AppImage — works on any distro.

What you get

📄 Paper Ingestion & Search

Upload PDFs, auto-extract text, create embeddings, and search across your library with meaning.

🤖 Research Chat (RAG)

Ask questions grounded on your selected papers. Fully offline SLM via llama.cpp.

✍️ Paper Writer + Live Preview

AI-assisted LaTeX editor with KaTeX live preview, auto-healing compiler, and compiled PDF export.

📊 Analysis & Gap Finder

Summarize papers, extract key claims, and discover under-explored research directions.

🔒 Encrypted Vault

Encrypt your papers locally with password-derived keys. Nothing is uploaded. Ever.

💻 Desktop & Web

Runs as a native desktop app (Tauri) or as a local web server. Your choice.
