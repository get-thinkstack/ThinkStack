Features

The parts of research that
eat your time, handled.

These tools run entirely on your machine, not in someone else's data center. No subscription to manage, and no server holding your papers that could get breached.

AI-Powered Paper Analysis

Drop in your PDFs and a local language model reads them, pulls out the key claims, and lets you search your whole collection by meaning instead of just keywords.

Research Gap Finder

It looks across what you've collected and flags the areas nobody's really covered yet, along with a few directions worth chasing.

AES-256 Encryption

AES-256-GCM with Argon2id key derivation. Your papers stay locked behind your password, and nobody else holds a key.

AI LaTeX Paper Writer

Write in a built-in Monaco editor with LaTeX compiling in real time, and let the AI help draft a section when you're stuck.

100% Offline & Private

All the AI work happens locally through llama.cpp. There's no API key to manage and nothing ever leaves your machine.

Peer-to-Peer Sharing

Share encrypted papers directly with collaborators over a P2P network built on libp2p. No server sitting in the middle.

How It Works

Getting from chaos to a draft.

It only takes three steps to go from a pile of PDFs to something you can actually write from.

1

Upload Your Papers

Drop your PDFs into ThinkStack and they get indexed and made searchable right away, all without leaving your computer.

2

The AI Reads Through It

A small local model reads through everything, summarizes it, and starts mapping how the papers relate to each other.

3

Discover & Write

From there you can see the gaps, get a few directions worth exploring, and start drafting in the built-in LaTeX editor.
