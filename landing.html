Features

Everything you need to
crush your research.

Powerful AI tools that run entirely on your machine. No cloud, no subscriptions, no data leaks.

AI-Powered Paper Analysis

Ingest PDFs and get instant summaries, key claims extraction, and semantic search across your entire collection. Powered by local language models.

Research Gap Finder

Automatically identify unexplored areas and get AI-suggested research directions based on your paper collection.

Military-Grade Encryption

AES-256-GCM encryption with Argon2id key derivation. Your papers stay private, locked with your password.

AI LaTeX Paper Writer

Write academic papers with a built-in Monaco editor, real-time LaTeX compilation, and AI-assisted drafting.

100% Offline & Private

All AI processing happens locally via llama.cpp. No cloud dependencies, no API keys, no data leaves your machine.

Peer-to-Peer Sharing

Securely share encrypted papers with collaborators over a direct P2P network powered by libp2p. No middlemen.
